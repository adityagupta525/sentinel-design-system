What laying the journey out end to end found

Two copy defects in docs/screens-source that no single screen shows, because each needs two screens — or two lines of one screen — side by side.

One number, told twice

On the rebalance screen the sentence reads “brings equity from 71% back to 58%” while the move card beneath it reads “Brings equity from 67% to 58%” (src/screens/Chat.tsx:280 and :283). Both are on screen at once. 71 is the figure the whole journey is built on — the trace, the attribution and the chips all say it — so 67 is the one that is wrong.

Sharma is called “her”

The second move reads “Redirect her ₹30,000 monthly SIP” (Chat.tsx:284, and again at :753) in a journey that says his holdings and his mandate everywhere else. Rule 4 asks for provenance under every figure; this is the same standard applied to a person. Meera is “her” correctly elsewhere, so this is a copy slip in the Sharma flow, not a convention.

Neither is fixed here

Both live in docs/screens-source, which is the Figma Make archive and is kept byte-for-byte as it was exported. They are corrected when the screens that carry them are built — screens 5 and 7 — and they are written down now so that the correction is deliberate rather than accidental.
