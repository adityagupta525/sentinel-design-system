There is no shared-element transition in this system. An artifact does not morph into a full-screen canvas — the canvas was removed from the product in v5 and --dur-canvas deleted in v11. If a screen you are building wants to “smart animate” a card into a page, the answer here is that the card grows where it sits and the thread keeps its scroll position. That is the continuity the transition would have been for, bought without a second scroller.

Both keyframes carry both fill, so the outgoing screen must be removed by the caller after 320ms — the keyframe holds it at −30% and does not unmount it. Slide only on a push, never on a tab change or a filter: a slide states “you have gone somewhere”, and lying about that is worse than no motion.
