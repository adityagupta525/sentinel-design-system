AllocationCard — Equity/Debt/Cash rows plus one 6px stacked bar in three bronze tints. Identity is the row label; colour only orders magnitude.
```jsx
<AllocationCard segments={[{label:'Equity',value:71,color:'var(--color-alloc-equity)'},{label:'Debt',value:24,color:'var(--color-alloc-debt)'},{label:'Cash',value:5,color:'var(--color-alloc-cash)'}]} />
```
Never a pie or donut.
